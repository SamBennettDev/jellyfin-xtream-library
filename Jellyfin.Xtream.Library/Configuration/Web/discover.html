Discover

Lists popular English movies and series from TMDB as two libraries, Discover Movies and Discover Shows, leaving out whatever Radarr and Sonarr already hold. Press play on a title and the plugin finds releases through Radarr or Sonarr, shows them in the client's version picker, and streams the one you pick through TorrServer, so you can scrub and resume.

Free from themoviedb.org, under Settings, API.
Keeps obscure titles out. Raise it for a shorter, better-known catalog.
Sends the release you play to Radarr or Sonarr, which download and import it. The placeholder is replaced by the real file in your library.
